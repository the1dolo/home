Who we are

On our previous journeys

1DOLO is BAPR 3-1D's brand identity for A.Y. '26-'27 — the result of our block's combined efforts and hardships, built on top of two years of branding that came before it.

This is our third year, and we're straying from the thrill and naivety of the pastels. Our theme is about pushing past what we thought we were capable of, evolving past expectations, and becoming the guidance that the fresh eyes and voices who were once us now look up to.
